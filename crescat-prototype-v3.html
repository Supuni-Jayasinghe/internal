Visiting
Colombo?

Everything you need to make Crescat part of your Colombo experience — directions, floors, and what to find where.

What's on each floor

BasementDining & Daily

Keells Supermarket anchors the basement with an exceptional cheese, wine, and imported foods selection. Coco Verandah, Sugar Bistro, The Wine Bar, and Brick Lane Cookie Co. complete the level.

Floor TwoFashion & Beauty

Women's fashion from Shopping Girl, Kadapatha, and Broadway. The Body Shop for natural beauty and skincare. Barefoot — one of Colombo's most iconic lifestyle stores — for handloom textiles, books, and gifts.

Floor ThreeStyle & Lifestyle

Hameedia for bespoke Sri Lankan menswear. Paris Gallery for international brands. Mlesna Tea for the finest Ceylon tea gifts. Chagall hair salon and Parfumerie round out the floor.

Good to know

Crescat is directly connected to Cinnamon Grand Colombo — hotel guests can walk straight into the mall. The Perahera Mawatha entrance is easiest from Galle Face.

On-site parking is available. Keells validates parking for purchases over Rs. 1,000. The food court stays open until 10pm — later than most malls in the city.

Getting Here

From your hotel

Adjacent to Cinnamon Grand. Guests of Shangri-La, Galle Face Hotel, and Hilton Colombo are a 5–10 minute tuk-tuk ride away.

By car

On Galle Road, Colombo 03 — near the junction of Perahera Mawatha. Enter from Perahera Mawatha for easiest parking access.

By tuk-tuk or taxi

Tell your driver "Crescat, Kollupitiya" or "next to Cinnamon Grand." PickMe and Uber drop directly at the entrance.

Est. 1997

Colombo's original
upmarket mall.

Crescat Boulevard opened in 1997 as Colombo's first curated upmarket retail destination — adjacent to what was then Hotel Lanka Oberoi, now Cinnamon Grand, pioneering the concept of an intimate neighbourhood mall for the discerning Colombo shopper.

After 25 years, a major refurbishment in 2021 renewed the space while preserving everything that made Crescat beloved: its intimacy, its carefully chosen tenant mix, and its unique position on Galle Road.

The Refurbishment

In November 2021, Crescat reopened after a comprehensive refurbishment by John Keells Properties. New interiors, a refreshed F&B lineup, and a reconsidered tenant mix — while retaining the warmth and familiarity that two decades of loyal customers had built their routines around.

Part of the JKH Family

Crescat Boulevard is managed by John Keells Properties, the property management arm of John Keells Holdings — Sri Lanka's largest listed conglomerate. As a property of Asian Hotels and Properties PLC (AHPL), Crescat sits alongside Cinnamon Grand Colombo and Cinnamon Lakeside.

The Cinnamon Grand is directly adjacent to the mall, giving Crescat a unique connection to one of Colombo's finest five-star hotels and its guest catchment — a combination no other Colombo mall can match.
